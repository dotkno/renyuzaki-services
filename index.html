05 · Notes

Things worth knowing

Read before booking
// 01

Parts are paid separately. If something needs replacing, you can buy it yourself or we can help you find one. The part is not part of our service fee.

// 02

You always know the price first. No surprise charges. You approve the price, then we do the work.

// 03

We do not do soldering repairs. We handle cleaning, upgrades, installation, and finding faults. If your unit needs soldering work, we will point you to a shop that does it.

// 04

Same problem back within 7 days? We look again for free. A new or different problem gets its own price.

// 05

Your files stay private. We do not open your files and we do not keep your passwords. Saved copies are deleted after you confirm everything works.
